Slot Myths Worth Dropping

A random number generator decides every spin, which makes the following beliefs harmless but useless.

  • a slot is "hot" or "cold" after a streak;
  • a big win is "due" after a long dry spell;
  • changing the bet size changes the RTP;
  • the time of day affects results;
  • stopping the reels early changes the outcome.
