The Lucky Draw

The Lucky Draw is attached to the first deposit bonus: first-time depositors take part in a draw with prizes up to 1,000. It does not replace the 50%; it comes on top. How and when prizes are allocated is described in the promotion card, and the result is shown inside the account.

How a Bonus Is Credited

The order of operations matters: claim first, deposit second.

  1. Open the promotions section and select the offer;
  2. Deposit the qualifying amount in the cashier;
  3. The bonus is credited according to the card;
  4. Play the eligible games until the wagering requirement is met;
  5. Withdraw once the requirement is complete.

If the bonus does not appear after the deposit, contact support before playing; wagering the deposit first can cancel the claim.

Wagering: Reading the Card

Five terms decide the real value of any offer.

Rules That Can Void a Bonus

Bonuses are lost for procedural reasons far more often than for anything else.

  • depositing before the offer was selected;
  • betting above the maximum bet while the bonus is active;
  • playing games excluded from the offer;
  • letting the validity window run out;
  • holding more than one account;
  • requesting a withdrawal before the wagering is complete.
